Do you take small jobs?
Yes. Hanging a mirror, swapping cabinet hardware, fixing one sticky door. Customers often mention that Korey is happy to take on small projects. You can also save up a few and knock out the whole list in one visit.
Are you insured?
Yes. Handyman of Dunedin LLC is a registered Florida business, fully insured, and accredited by the Better Business Bureau with an A rating.
How does pricing work?
Korey looks at the job and gives you a clear estimate before any work starts. If something unexpected comes up once the work is underway, he checks with you before changing anything.
Can you build something custom for my home?
That's Korey's favorite kind of job. Built-in seating, bookcases, floating shelves, media consoles, outdoor showers and planters are all things he has built for local homes. Call with your idea and a rough size, and he'll talk it through with you.
Which areas do you cover?
Dunedin, Clearwater, Palm Harbor, Safety Harbor, Belleair and Belleair Beach in Pinellas County. If you're close by but not on that list, call and ask.
How can I pay?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, debit cards, cash, checks, PayPal and Venmo.
